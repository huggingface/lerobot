**State-of-the-art machine learning for real-world robotics**

🤗 LeRobot is Hugging Face's open-source library for teaching robots new skills with AI. You show your robot a task a few dozen times, LeRobot records it, trains a **policy** (the AI model that controls the robot) on those recordings, and runs it on the robot.

It works with low-cost arms like the SO-101 as well as mobile robots and humanoids, and every dataset and policy can be shared on the [Hugging Face Hub](https://huggingface.co/lerobot), where thousands are already available.

## How it works

1. **Teleoperate:** control the robot yourself, usually by moving a second "leader" arm with your hand.
2. **Record:** do the task a few dozen times. Each try is saved as an episode: the camera videos plus the robot's movements.
3. **Train:** a policy learns to copy what you did.
4. **Run:** the policy controls the robot and does the task on its own.

New to robot learning? [How robot learning works](./how_it_works) explains this loop and the words you'll meet, in five minutes.
