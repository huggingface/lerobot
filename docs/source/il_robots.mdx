# Teach your robot a task

In this tutorial, you teach your robot a task by showing it how. You do the task a few dozen times with the leader arm while LeRobot records it, train a [policy](./glossary#policy) on those recordings, and the policy then does the task on its own. This way of teaching a robot is called [imitation learning](./glossary#imitation-learning). You don't need any machine learning experience.

The tutorial works with any [supported robot](./robots). The commands are shown for the SO-101, and an **Other robots** tab shows the same command for any other robot.

## What you need

- **A robot and a way to control it:** usually a follower arm and a leader arm, like the SO-101. Don't have one yet? See [Choose a robot](./robots).
- **One or two USB cameras.**
- **A computer** with LeRobot [installed](./installation), on Linux, macOS or Windows.
- **A GPU to train on:** your own NVIDIA GPU, a Mac with Apple Silicon (slower), a free Google Colab GPU, or a GPU rented on Hugging Face Jobs. See [Which GPU do I need?](./hardware_guide).
- **A free [Hugging Face account](https://huggingface.co/join)**, to store your dataset and your policy.

## The steps

| Step                                            | What you do                                                    | Time                               |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| [1. Set up your robot](./setup_your_robot)      | Build or unbox your robot, connect it and calibrate it.        | 15 minutes to an afternoon         |
| [2. Teleoperate and add cameras](./teleoperate) | Control the robot with the leader arm, and place your cameras. | 30 minutes                         |
| [3. Record a dataset](./record_dataset)         | Record about 50 episodes of your task.                         | About an hour                      |
| [4. Check your data](./check_dataset)           | Watch and replay your episodes, and remove the bad ones.       | 15 minutes                         |
| [5. Train a policy](./train_policy)             | Train a policy on your dataset.                                | 1 to 8 hours, depending on the GPU |
| [6. Run it on your robot](./run_policy)         | Let the policy do the task, and count how often it succeeds.   | 30 minutes                         |
| [7. Make it work better](./improve_policy)      | Find out why it fails, and fix it.                             | As needed                          |

New to robot learning? [How robot learning works](./how_it_works) explains the words used in this tutorial in five minutes.

> [!TIP]
> **Prefer not to use the command line?** [LeLab](./lelab) does the same steps in your browser, for the SO-101. **Prefer a notebook?** The [quickstart notebook](https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot/blob/main/examples/notebooks/quickstart.ipynb) [![Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/assets/colab-badge.svg)](https://colab.research.google.com/github/huggingface/lerobot/blob/main/examples/notebooks/quickstart.ipynb) asks for your robot's settings once and writes every command for you.
