# Choose a robot

LeRobot works with many robots, from small arms you build yourself to humanoids. **For your first robot, we recommend the SO-101:** it's low-cost and open-source, it's the robot most LeRobot users have (so most shared datasets and community help are for it), and every example in these docs uses it.

Not ready to get a robot? [Try LeRobot without one](./quickstart) first, or learn in [simulation](./simulation_overview).

## What to look at

- **Build or buy.** Kits you assemble yourself cost less, and you learn how the robot works, but take an afternoon or more. Ready-made robots work out of the box.
- **A leader arm.** To record episodes, you need a way to control the robot: most arms come with a matching leader arm, which is the easiest way.
- **How it connects.** Robots that connect over USB work on Linux, macOS and Windows. Robots that use a CAN adapter work only on Linux and need a bit more setup.
- **One arm or two.** Two arms (a bimanual setup) can do more tasks, but cost twice as much and need more data.
