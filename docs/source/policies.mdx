# Choose a policy

A [policy](./glossary#policy) is the AI model that controls your robot. LeRobot has more than 25 of them. **Start with [ACT](./act):** it's small, it trains in about an hour on a gaming GPU, and it learns one task well from about 50 episodes. For a single task, it's often as good as policies many times its size.

## Which one should I use?

| If you…                                                                              | Use                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Are training your first policy, or want one task done well                           | [ACT](./act)                                                                         |
| Want one policy for several tasks, that follows a text instruction, with a 16 GB GPU | [SmolVLA](./smolvla), fine-tuned from its pretrained version                         |
| Have a GPU with 24 GB or more, and want the strongest pretrained policies            | A large VLA such as [π₀.₅](./pi05), [GR00T](./groot) or [X-VLA](./xvla)              |
| Want to evaluate on simulation benchmarks or reproduce published results             | The policy's own page: most have recipes for [LIBERO](./libero) and other benchmarks |
| Work on research into world models                                                   | A world action model (see the table below)                                           |

Bigger isn't automatically better: large policies need more GPU memory, train more slowly and run more slowly on the robot. Get a working result with ACT first, then try a bigger policy with the same dataset to see if it helps.

## The three kinds of policies

- **Small imitation-learning models** learn from scratch on your episodes. They're small and fast, and usually learn one task at a time. [ACT](./act) and [Diffusion Policy](./diffusion) are the classic choices; [Multitask DiT](./multi_task_dit) also reads a text instruction, to learn several tasks.
- **Vision-language-action models (VLAs)** start from a policy already trained on many robots and tasks, and read a text instruction as well as the cameras. You [fine-tune](./glossary#fine-tune) them on your episodes, which needs fewer episodes per task but a larger GPU.
- **World action models (WAMs)** predict what the cameras will see next as well as the actions to take. They are large, mostly used in research, and most of their guides show results on simulation benchmarks.

## All policies

- **GPU memory** is for training, as measured in [Which GPU do I need?](./hardware_guide). "Large" means the policy wasn't measured there: see its page.
- **Guide shows** tells you what the policy's page demonstrates: a run on a real robot, results in simulation, or training only.

## Learning by trial and error

All the policies above learn from your episodes. LeRobot can also train a policy by trial and error, with reinforcement learning: see [Reinforcement learning: when to use it](./rl_overview).
