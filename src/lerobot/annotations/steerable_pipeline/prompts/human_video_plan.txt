You are preparing a human demonstration of one step of a robot manipulation video.

The {n} images are frames sampled uniformly from the robot's segment ({duration:.1f} s); image 1 is its first frame and image {n} its last.
Subtask performed in this segment (authoritative): "{subtask}"
Episode instruction (context only): "{task}"

Return one JSON object with exactly these keys:
- "human_task": the subtask phrased for a person, naming the hand(s) that act (the hand on the same image side as the robot arm that acts), e.g. "Pick up the red cup with the right hand and place it on the tray." or "Fold the towel in half with both hands."
- "object": short name and appearance of the manipulated object.
- "target": short name and appearance of where it goes (or "" if there is none).
- "hand": "left" or "right" when one robot arm moves objects in this segment, or "both" only when both robot arms or grippers actively manipulate objects in it (e.g. folding, a two-handed lift, a handover). An arm that only waits or holds its pose does not count.
- "steps": list of 2 to 4 short steps describing how the objects move during the segment.
- "final_state": one short phrase describing the final object state.
- "edit_prompt": an instruction for an image-editing model that turns image 1 into a first-person view of a person doing the task. It must: (1) completely remove every robot arm, gripper, cable and robot base, filling those areas with the continuing table and background; (2) add exactly two realistic human hands and forearms (the viewer's own) entering from the bottom edge and resting relaxed on the near edge of the table, touching nothing; (3) list every object that must stay exactly as it is, with colour and position; (4) keep the camera viewpoint, table, background, lighting and layout unchanged and photorealistic. It must not add any other people, arms or objects.
- "end_edit_prompt": the same kind of instruction for image {n}, the segment's last frame: remove the robot completely, add the viewer's two hands resting relaxed on the near edge of the table touching nothing, and keep every object exactly where it is in image {n} (list them with colour and position), with the same camera, table, background and lighting.
