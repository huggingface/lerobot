frames above are grouped and numbered "Camera 1", "Camera 2", ... in order — each

For EACH camera decide two things.

1. QUALITY. Is the view usable for training a policy? Mark it UNUSABLE if EITHER:
   - it has a genuine capture defect: blurry / out of focus, badly over- or
     under-exposed, static/frozen, corrupted, or otherwise not showing the scene
     clearly; OR
   - a human is visible in the frame. These are teleoperated recordings and the
     human operator must never appear — any visible person, human hand, arm, or
     body part (as opposed to the robot) makes the view unusable.
   Otherwise it is usable. Do NOT mark a view unusable because the robot's own
   gripper, arm, or end-effector is visible or partially blocks it — that is
   EXPECTED, especially for a wrist-mounted camera. Carefully distinguish the
   robot (a metal/plastic manipulator) from a human limb.

2. VIEW LABEL. Where does this camera sit relative to the robot? Use ONLY this
   closed vocabulary:

     {vocabulary}

   - front  : the camera faces the robot head-on — a frontal view of the robot.
   - rear   : the camera is behind the robot, looking at its back.
   - side   : the camera views the robot from the side. ALWAYS qualify it with
              the side it is on — "left_side" or "right_side".
   - top    : the camera looks down on the workspace from above (overhead).
   - bottom : the camera looks up at the workspace from below.
   - wrist  : the camera is mounted on the robot's arm/gripper and moves with it;
              qualify with the arm when there are two — "left_wrist" / "right_wrist".

   Compare them and give each a DISTINCT label — only assign two cameras the same
